Kazi & Enterprise

NYOTA Program Rollout

Funding Structure: Beneficiaries receive their start-up packages split into allocations—typically KES 22,000 sent to mobile wallets (like Pochi la Biashara) for immediate business operations and KES 3,000 directed into NSSF Haba na Haba savings accounts.Phase Two Rollout: Covered 27 counties (including Nairobi, Kajiado, Nakuru, Machakos, Kiambu, Uasin Gishu, and others) with regional disbursements and mandatory mentorship programs.Nationwide Reach: The government has progressively pushed to reach over 122,000 youth in recent tranches, aiming to scale up to over 800,000 young Kenyans under the broader initiative.Support Measures: President William Ruto has directed county governments to waive business permit fees for NYOTA beneficiaries for two years and prioritize allocating market spaces for youth enterprises.How to Apply or Check StatusUSSD Code: Eligible youth aged 18–29 (and up to 35 for Persons with Disabilities) can access details or apply by dialing *254# and selecting the NYOTA Project option.Support Contacts: For official queries, you can reach out via the toll-free line at 0800 222 221 or visit local County/Sub-County offices of the State Department for Youth Affairs and Creative Economy.

Elimu (Higher Ed)

EDUCATION

The New Funding Model: Kenya Kwanza replaced block funding to universities with a student-centered model categorized into 5 distinct financial bands. Funding is allocated based on a means-testing instrument that assesses family vulnerability.Targeted Scholarships & Loans: Under this model, the most vulnerable students (Band 1) receive up to 95% government support through a combination of tuition scholarships (70%) and Higher Education Loans Board (HELB) loans (25%), requiring minimal household contributions. Higher bands shift larger shares to loans and household payments.Student Village Hostels (Affordable Housing)Institutional Housing Program: Recognizing an accommodation crisis in higher learning institutions, the administration integrated student residences into the Affordable Housing Programme. Over 177,000 student hostel beds are planned or actively under construction across the country.Massive Campus Investments: Major multi-billion shilling funding has been dedicated to universities, KMTCs, and TVETs. Projects include a Ksh 1.17 Billion development at Alupe University (1,025 units), a Ksh 640 Million project at Kibabii University, and dedicated hostel blocks at JKUAT and Maseno University..
